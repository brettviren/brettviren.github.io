Experiments

Experiments I collaborate on.

  • DUNE
    The Deep Underground Neutrino Experiment.
  • MicroBooNE
    A liquid argon time projection chamber neutrino experiment in the Booster Neutrino Beam at Fermilab.
  • Daya Bay
    A reactor antineutrino experiment in China that measured the neutrino mixing angle θ13.
  • MINOS
    A long-baseline neutrino oscillation experiment in Fermilab’s NuMI beam with a far detector in the Soudan mine.
  • Super-Kamiokande
    A large water Cherenkov detector in the Kamioka mine, Japan, studying neutrinos and searching for proton decay.

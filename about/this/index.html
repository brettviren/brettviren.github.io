About this site

2021-10-02 · updated 2026-11-25 · meta · tags: org, hugo · org source · version v0-0-gb998507

In which I give the requisite grandiloquent soliloquy about how this site is made.

Purpose and intent #

Many times in the past I have attempted to make a “web site” that is somehow about me. This is the latest. Time will tell if it survives.

This site is intended as an outlet for:

  • Provide browsable, searchable collection of talks.

  • Distribution of Totally Meaningful Parts (tmp).

  • I will sometimes answer technical questions in a blog post, in a longer and potentially revised article (such as howido) or with LLM-assistance an artifact. Some such things may also land in Wire-Cell News posts.

  • I may occasiounly put a short non sequitur or a “today I learned” in notes.

Organization #

Following Hugo nomenclature the site is organized first by sections. Sections are interpreted as a type or a form of page. The following sections may be found:

posts
short “blog” entries, unlikely to (but may) see updates.
articles
long form on a topic and potentially updated over time.
talks
slides from presentations I have given, each with a summary.
notes
things likely more interesting to myself than others which is meant to summarize or simply mark some occurrence.
projects
one page for each software project I develop or contribute to.
experiments
one page for each experiment I collaborate on.
about
meta descriptions (ie, this page).

Pages are further labeled by taxonomies. Hugo provides these with no assumed interpretation and so these are taken:

categories
A limited number of general descriptors defined in a top-down manner. These label the basic topical subject of the page independent of its section.
tags
Numerous, unbound set of detailed descriptors defined in a bottom-up manner as pages are written. These label aspects of the content regardless of category or section.
groups and topics
The group (eg, an experiment or project) to which a talk was given and the topic of the talk.

Each project and experiment page automatically lists the pages whose tag, group or topic matches it. The search page searches all pages from within your browser.

The site is served in two places. My personal site holds pages of personal interest and those of general interest. My work site holds pages of work interest, those of general interest and the talks. Some pages are only readable by members of a group.

Tech #

This site uses GNU Emacs Org Mode with ox-hugo to make content for Hugo. See howido if interested in details of my personal computing configuration. The org source of each page is linked from it (“org source”). Writing, building and deploying the site is done with myweb, a small command line tool I wrote that keeps the sources in a DataLad dataset, sorts pages to each site by their audience and deploys each by rsync or git. Though not likely interesting to humans, the exported HTML of the personal site lands in this repo.

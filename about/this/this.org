* Purpose and intent
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: purpose-and-intent
:END:

Many times in the past I have attempted to make a "web site" that is
somehow [[/about/me][about me]].  This is the latest.  Time will tell if it survives.

This site is intended as an outlet for:

- Provide browsable, searchable collection of [[/talks][talks]].

- Distribution of Totally Meaningful Parts ([[https://www.phy.bnl.gov/~bviren/tmp/][tmp]]).

- I will sometimes answer technical questions in a [[/posts][blog]] post, in a longer and
  potentially revised [[/articles][article]] (such as [[/articles/howido][howido]]) or with LLM-assistance an
  [[site:artifacts/][artifact]].  Some such things may also land in [[https://wirecell.github.io/news/][Wire-Cell News]] posts.

- I may occasiounly put a short non sequitur or a "today I learned" in [[/notes][notes]].

* Organization
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: organization
:END:

Following Hugo nomenclature the site is organized first by /sections/.
Sections are interpreted as a type or a form of page.  The following
sections may be found:

- [[/posts][posts]] :: short "blog" entries, unlikely to (but may) see updates.
- [[/articles][articles]] :: long form on a topic and potentially updated over time.
- [[/talks][talks]] :: slides from presentations I have given, each with a summary.
- [[/notes][notes]] :: things likely more interesting to myself than others which is meant to summarize or simply mark some occurrence.
- [[/projects][projects]] :: one page for each software project I develop or contribute to.
- [[/experiments][experiments]] :: one page for each experiment I collaborate on.
- [[/about][about]] :: meta descriptions (ie, this page).

Pages are further labeled by /taxonomies/.  Hugo provides these with no
assumed interpretation and so these are taken:

- [[/categories][categories]] :: A limited number of general descriptors defined in a top-down manner.  These label the basic topical subject of the page independent of its section.
- [[/tags][tags]] :: Numerous, unbound set of detailed descriptors defined in a bottom-up manner as pages are written.  These label aspects of the content regardless of category or section.
- [[/groups][groups]] and [[/topics][topics]] :: The group (eg, an experiment or project) to which a talk was given and the topic of the talk.

Each project and experiment page automatically lists the pages whose
tag, group or topic matches it.  The [[/search][search]] page searches all
pages from within your browser.

The site is served in two places.  My [[https://brettviren.github.io/][personal site]] holds pages of
personal interest and those of general interest.  My [[https://www.phy.bnl.gov/~bviren/][work site]] holds
pages of work interest, those of general interest and the talks.  Some
pages are only readable by members of a group.

* Tech
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: tech
:END:

This site uses [[https://www.gnu.org/software/emacs/][GNU Emacs]] [[https://orgmode.org/][Org Mode]] with [[https://ox-hugo.scripter.co/][ox-hugo]] to make content for
[[https://gohugo.io/][Hugo]].  See [[/articles/howido][howido]] if interested in details of my personal computing
configuration.  The org source of each page is linked from it ("org
source").  Writing, building and deploying the site is done with
[[https://github.com/brettviren/myweb][myweb]], a small command line tool I wrote that keeps the sources in a
[[https://www.datalad.org/][DataLad]] dataset, sorts pages to each site by their audience and
deploys each by rsync or git.  Though not likely interesting to humans,
the exported HTML of the personal site lands in [[https://github.com/brettviren/brettviren.github.io][this repo]].
